# Security policy

Report a vulnerability in any Opaq repository, in opaq.computer or in the Opaq API to **team@opaq.computer**.

Please include what you found, where, the steps to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. A short proof of concept helps. Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

**In scope:** opaq.computer, the console, the API under `/v1` and `/api`, the way sealed top-ups are credited, and the code in this organization.

**Out of scope:** the Privacy Rings protocol itself (report it to Helius), third-party model providers, denial of service and volume tests, social engineering.

**Safe harbor:** research done in good faith, without touching other people's data or degrading the service, and reported to us before anyone else, will not face legal action from Opaq. With your permission, we credit you in the [changelog](https://opaq.computer/changelog).
